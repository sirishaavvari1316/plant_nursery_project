🦠 Common Plant Diseases

← Back to Explore Plants
🍂

Leaf Spot

Brown or black spots appear on plant leaves.

Solution: Remove affected leaves and avoid excessive moisture.

🤍

Powdery Mildew

White powder-like growth appears on leaves.

Solution: Improve air circulation and avoid watering the leaves.

🌿

Root Rot

Roots become dark and soft because of excess water.

Solution: Reduce watering and use well-draining soil.

🟠

Rust Disease

Orange or reddish spots may appear on leaves.

Solution: Remove infected leaves and keep plants well ventilated.

🥀

Wilt Disease

Leaves become weak, droopy and may turn yellow.

Solution: Check watering, roots and soil drainage.

🦠

Downy Mildew

Yellow patches and greyish growth can appear on leaves.

Solution: Keep leaves dry and provide good air circulation.
